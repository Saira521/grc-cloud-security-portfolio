## 1. Control assertion

What exactly does the control claim happens?

## 2. Design effectiveness

- Is the control appropriately designed to address the stated risk?
- Are scope, owner, frequency, criteria, and exception handling defined?
- Design conclusion: Effective / Partially Effective / Ineffective

## 3. Implementation

- Is the control actually deployed in the scoped process/system?
- What evidence proves implementation?
- Implementation conclusion: Effective / Partially Effective / Ineffective

## 4. Operating effectiveness

### Population

Define the full population for the review period.

### Sample selection

Explain the sampling approach. Include ordinary and exception-path samples where relevant.

### Test attributes

- performed on time
- correct population/scope
- correct owner/reviewer
- required approval present
- exceptions identified
- remediation completed
- evidence retained

### Exceptions

Record deviations and whether they are isolated or systemic.

### Operating conclusion

Effective / Partially Effective / Ineffective

## 5. Risk impact

- Does the control effectiveness rating change residual likelihood?
- Does it change residual impact?
- Is risk treatment required?

## 6. Final conclusion

Summarize why the control receives its final effectiveness rating.
